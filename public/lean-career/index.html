Lean Career

Lean Career helps Parker Hamilton discover relevant jobs, assess their fit, keep track of applications and deadlines, and prepare application documents for opportunities he personally selects.

It runs as a private, owner-operated application. This page provides information about the app; it is not a public sign-up or a portal to career records.

Why it connects to Google

  • Google Drive, read-only: retrieve configured career source files and existing collector publications.
  • Google Sheets, read-only: read existing application history to avoid duplicate work.
  • Gmail, read-only: identify relevant employer correspondence and upcoming actions.

These permissions do not allow the Google connection to edit files or spreadsheets, send email, or change mailbox contents. The owner decides which jobs to pursue. Lean Career does not automatically contact employers or submit applications.

AI assistance

When enabled by the owner, AI services assess vacancies and draft or review application documents using relevant job information and career evidence. The default service is the OpenAI API; DeepSeek is an optional configured alternative. Google data access and AI data handling are explained in the Lean Career Privacy Policy.

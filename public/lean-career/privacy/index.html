Lean Career

Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026.

Lean Career is a personal career-management application operated by Parker Hamilton for his own use. This policy explains how the application accesses, uses, stores and shares data. The public information pages do not provide access to the private application or its records.

Google data accessed

The Google connection requests only Drive read-only, Sheets read-only and Gmail read-only permissions. These permissions can authorize reading files, spreadsheets and email in the connected account; the application's implemented reads are limited to its configured career sources and relevant employer communications.

  • Drive: configured career documents, existing job-collector publications, file identifiers and metadata.
  • Sheets: application-history rows and job identities needed to reconcile records and prevent duplicate applications.
  • Gmail: the connected account's email address, matching message identifiers, sender, subject, date and snippets; relevant message text where needed to identify actions or deadlines.

The connection does not request permission to send email, edit or delete mail, or modify Google Drive files or spreadsheets.

Purpose and use

Data is used to discover and assess job opportunities, avoid duplicate work, maintain the owner's career records, identify employer communications and deadlines, prepare daily reports, and support application documents for jobs the owner selects. The application does not automatically contact employers or submit applications.

AI services and sharing

When the owner enables AI screening, drafting or review, relevant job information and career evidence may be sent to the configured AI API provider to perform that task. This can include relevant excerpts from authorized career material. The default provider is OpenAI; DeepSeek is an optional alternative selected through configuration. These requests are subject to the selected provider's API data-handling and retention terms. The current Gmail refresh and message classification use deterministic code and do not send mailbox contents to an AI provider as part of that refresh.

Lean Career does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train or improve generalized AI or machine-learning models. Google data is not published on this website. Private source files and optional reports or application documents may remain in the owner's private Google Drive or local storage. Any further sharing requires the owner's direction, except where necessary to comply with law or protect security.

Lean Career's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage, protection and retention

The application stores working records, cached assessments, source snapshots and generated documents on the owner's computer, including its local SQLite database. OAuth credentials are stored separately in the owner's local configuration, not in these public pages or the website repository. Connections to Google and the configured AI API use HTTPS. Local files and private Drive content depend on the owner's device, account and backup protections.

Career records and caches are retained until the owner deletes them. Backups and private Drive copies may persist until separately removed. AI providers may retain API request data according to their applicable service terms; deleting local records does not itself delete provider-held data.

Revoking access and deleting data

The owner can stop the application and revoke its Google access through Google Account connections. Revocation prevents further authorized access but does not erase previously saved records. To remove those records, the owner can delete the application's local database, snapshots, generated documents and local OAuth credentials, and any corresponding private Drive copies and backups.

These public pages

These pages contain no sign-in, forms, application data, analytics scripts or advertising. The website host, Cloudflare, may process standard request information such as IP addresses and browser details to deliver and secure the website. Search engines are instructed not to index these pages; they remain publicly accessible to anyone with the URLs.

Contact and changes

Questions or deletion requests can be directed to Parker Hamilton at parkerhamilton5324@gmail.com. This policy will be updated if data practices change. New uses of Google data or broader permissions require updated disclosure and the owner's consent before they are enabled.
